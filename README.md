# 🎓 StudyHub AI

> An intelligent, all-in-one academic companion designed to eliminate study friction and give students hours of their time back.

---

## 📌 The Problem

Modern students spend up to **40% of their study time** wrestling with logistics instead of actually learning:
* **Scattered Sourcing:** Spending hours scouring databases to find textbooks, research papers, and trusted academic sources.
* **Tedious Research & Synthesis:** Getting stuck in deep dives, manual reading, and cross-referencing without clear structure.
* **Math & Science Note Friction:** Wasting valuable time manually typing complex equations and formatting $\LaTeX$ syntax after lectures.

---

## 💡 The Solution: StudyHub AI

**StudyHub AI** automates tedious study workflows so you can focus on deep comprehension and high performance.

### ✨ Key Features

* 📚 **Smart Book & Literature Finder:** Quickly pinpoint required textbooks, reference chapters, academic papers, and reliable study materials without paywall/search fatigue.
* 🔬 **AI Research Assistant:** Summarize complex literature, extract core methodologies, generate bibliographies, and synthesize multi-source findings in seconds.
* 🎙️ **Voice-to-$\LaTeX$ Converter:** Speak complex formulas, integrals, and proofs naturally—StudyHub transcribes your spoken math directly into clean, formatted $\LaTeX$ code ready for papers and notes.
* ⚡ **Study Session Accelerator:** Convert notes into flashcards, generate practice quizzes, and organize course materials by topic.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** Vite, React, TypeScript
* **Backend / Database:** Firebase / Cloud Firestore
* **Runtime & Package Manager:** Bun / Node.js
* **AI & NLP:** Multimodal LLMs, Speech-to-Text & Math Formatting Engine
